Major Haemorrhage Procedure

Emergency Department Quick Reference Guide

TRIGGER: Bleeding with haemodynamic compromise

  • Heart rate greater than 110 bpm.
  • Systolic blood pressure less than 90 mmHg.

1. Activate MHP

  • Call switchboard on 2222.
  • State "I want to activate the major haemorrhage procedure".
  • Provide your exact location and extension.
  • Nominate a team leader.
  • Nominate a communications coordinator to liaise with the transfusion laboratory.

2. Immediate Actions

  • Take bloods: Send VBG or ABG, FBC, U&E, calcium, INR, APTT, fibrinogen, and Group and Screen.
  • Transfuse components: Take emergency blood from the ED fridge. Transfuse 4 units of group O red cells and 4 units of thawed plasma.
  • Give tranexamic acid (TXA):
    • Trauma (under 3 hours): Give 1 g IV bolus over 10 minutes followed by 1 g infusion over 8 hours.
    • Other bleeding: Give 1 g bolus.
    • Do not give routinely for GI bleeding.

3. Ongoing Support

  • Warm the patient: Apply active warming blankets. Keep patient temperature above 36 degrees Celsius.
  • Replace calcium: Give 10 ml of 10% calcium chloride after every 4 units of red cells and plasma.
  • Reassess: Retake bloods every 30 to 60 minutes.
  • No results yet? If the patient is not fluid resuscitated and results are unavailable, order and give the next MHP pack.

4. Guide Therapy with Lab Results

  • Hb falling or under 80 g/L: Give red cells.
  • Platelets under 75: Give platelets (use a threshold of 100 for traumatic brain injury).
  • INR or APTT ratio over 1.5: Give FFP.
  • Fibrinogen under 1.5 g/L: Give cryoprecipitate.

Special Situations

Patients on anticoagulants (DOACs/warfarin)
  • Call the haematologist on-call immediately.
  • Give prothrombin complex concentrate (Beriplex) per guidelines.
  • Do not wait for lab results to reverse anticoagulation.
Patients on antiplatelets
  • Give 1 unit of platelets as soon as possible.
Pregnant patients (obstetric haemorrhage)
  • Target fibrinogen is higher at 2.0 g/L Give cryoprecipitate if levels fall below this.
  • Consider early use of cryoprecipitate.
  • Call the obstetric consultant on-call immediately.

Stand Down

  • Call the transfusion laboratory to stand down once bleeding stops.
  • Return unused products to the lab immediately.
  • Complete all traceability documentation.
  • Assess the patient for thromboprophylaxis.
CRITICAL SAFETY NOTICE: All patients must wear an identification wristband. Always check components against the patient wristband before administration. Group O positive blood must NOT be given to individuals with child-bearing potential whose blood group is unknown.
Based on UHB Blood Transfusion Procedure: Major Haemorrhage Procedure (MHP) CG1320, Version 1.3 (Aug 2024).
